About SmartMatch

SmartMatch is an innovative internship and job-matching platform built for the Department of Computer Application, Nandi Institute of Management & Science (NIMS). The project combines classical string matching algorithms (Knuth-Morris-Pratt & Rabin-Karp) with Dynamic Programming (Longest Common Subsequence) to automate resume screening and eliminate manual placement friction.
